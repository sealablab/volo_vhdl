QUICK ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY:

VoloApp = 3-layer hardware abstraction:
1. MCC_TOP_volo_loader.vhd (static, shared)
2. <AppName>_volo_shim.vhd (generated from Pydantic)
3. <AppName>_volo_main.vhd (hand-written app logic)

Register Map:
- CR0[31:29] = VOLO_READY control (3-bit ready scheme)
- CR10-CR14 = BRAM loader protocol (4KB buffer)
- CR20-CR30 = App registers (human-friendly interface)

NAMING CONVENTIONS (MANDATORY):
- Files: <AppName>_volo_{shim|main}.vhd
- Signals: "Pulse Width" → pulse_width (snake_case)
- Location: modules/<AppName>/volo_main/

KEY PRINCIPLE:
